# 📊 Marketing Campaign Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze the performance of marketing campaigns across different channels and regions. The dashboard provides insights into campaign effectiveness, return on investment (ROI), spending patterns, revenue generation, and customer engagement metrics.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze campaign performance across multiple marketing channels
- Compare Digital and Traditional marketing campaigns
- Monitor key business metrics such as Revenue, Spend, Clicks, and ROI
- Identify high-performing campaigns and regions
- Support data-driven marketing decisions through interactive visualizations

## 💡 Key Insights

### Campaign Performance
- **Influencer Marketing** achieved the highest ROI among all campaigns
- **Social Media** and **Search Engine** campaigns also delivered strong returns
- **TV Commercials** generated the lowest ROI compared to other channels

### Marketing Mix
- Digital campaigns represented the **majority** of all campaigns run
- Digital marketing channels consistently produced **better ROI performance** than traditional channels

### Regional Analysis
- **Africa** generated the highest revenue contribution
- **South America** recorded the lowest revenue among all regions
- Revenue exceeded marketing spend **across all regions**, indicating universally positive returns
